This content is suitable for all age groups, universally applicable and not gender-specific, and relevant for any relationship status and for individuals with or without kids. The target audience is currently focused on improving their relationships, seeking clarity about their life or career, desiring to grow and be their best self, and loving learning new things. They are motivated by achieving goals with less stress, being a better friend/partner/parent, finding direction and purpose, and making better life choices. They identify as individuals who focus on logic, analysis, and planning, are driven by values, emotion, and doing what is right, and love exploring new ideas and envisioning future possibilities. The concepts require either 10 min – A daily story + learning or 15+ min – Deep dive into wisdom, aligning best with a learner who loves deep thinking and reflection.
